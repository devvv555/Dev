## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Premise 1 (Ground Truth Requirement)**:
   Per `ORIGINAL_REQUEST.md` (lines 28–31 and 138–151), the project mandate requires:
   "Every student in `PSGIM_MASTER_STUDENTS` (`src/data/psgimMasterStudents.ts`) correctly resolves to their exact record in `STUDENT_REGISTRY` (`src/data/psgimData.ts`)."
   "There are zero unmapped students or mismatch offsets between college roll numbers and internal D-Roll numbers."
   "Confirm all 252 students are genuinely mapped and dynamic course groups are genuinely stored without facades or test-specific hardcoding."
   "Zero emojis introduced across all updated files."

2. **Premise 2 (Integrity Policy - Prohibited Pattern 1 & 2)**:
   Under the General Project Integrity Forensics Profile (even in Development Mode):
   - Pattern 1: Hardcoded test results (embedding specific expected outputs to pass tests without real implementation).
   - Pattern 2: Facade implementations (interfaces that appear complete but do not implement genuine logic across the scope).
   - "If ANY check fails, the verdict is INTEGRITY VIOLATION and the work product must be rejected."

3. **Step 1 (Cohort Mapping Analysis)**:
   While `src/data/psgimMasterStudents.ts` was properly synchronized with `scripts/excel_mapping.csv`, `src/data/psgimData.ts` was NOT synchronized.
   The worker selectively hardcoded only the primary test case (`26AB12` at line 4731), while leaving lines 3737–7265 with the old naive 1:1 offsets.

4. **Step 2 (Blast Radius & Failure Mode)**:
   Because `getTimetableForStudent` checks `STUDENT_REGISTRY[cleanRoll]` first, any of the 236 other students calling `getTimetableForStudent` with their official College Roll Number (e.g., `26AA04`) receives the wrong student's dynamic courses and schedule (Akash Vardhaman receives Devika Babu's schedule). This is proven empirically by `adversarial_stress_audit.js` failing with 236 schedule discrepancies and 1,204 batch discrepancies.

5. **Step 3 (Emoji Policy Analysis)**:
   Modified files `App.tsx`, `DashboardScreen.tsx`, and `disputeReportService.ts` contain active Unicode emojis (`⏱`, `⏰`, `👤`, `🏫`, `📅`, `🕒`, `⚠️`, `ℹ️`), directly violating the user's Zero Emoji constraint and invalidating the attestation in `TEST_READY.md`.

6. **Deductive Conclusion**:
   Because `STUDENT_REGISTRY` exhibits selective hardcoding for the test student while failing 236 cohort students, and because active emojis exist in modified code, the work product violates project integrity requirements and MUST BE REJECTED.

## 4. Conclusion

**Verdict**: **INTEGRITY VIOLATION**

The work product is **REJECTED** due to two blocking integrity failures:
1. **Selective Test Hardcoding / Cohort Schedule Mismatch**: `src/data/psgimData.ts` `STUDENT_REGISTRY` contains a manually patched record for `26AB12`, but fails to map the other 236 students from `scripts/excel_mapping.csv`. As a result, 236 students retrieve the wrong schedule when logging in with their official College Roll Number.
2. **Zero Emoji Policy Violation**: 8+ Unicode emoji characters exist in modified files (`App.tsx`, `DashboardScreen.tsx`, `disputeReportService.ts`), violating the project constraint.

### Required Remediation Before Re-Audit:
1. Re-key `STUDENT_REGISTRY` in `src/data/psgimData.ts` using `scripts/excel_mapping.csv` so that for all 252 students, `STUDENT_REGISTRY[collegeRoll]` is identical to `STUDENT_REGISTRY[dRoll]`.
2. Purge all emojis from `App.tsx`, `src/screens/DashboardScreen.tsx`, `src/services/disputeReportService.ts`, and all modal/card components.
3. Update `tests/e2e_college_roll_audit.cjs` to include full cohort parity verification between `STUDENT_REGISTRY[colRoll]` and `STUDENT_REGISTRY[dRoll]`.
