## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Step 1 (Ground Truth Baseline)**: The project request and `ORIGINAL_REQUEST.md` (2026-10-30T16:50:27Z) mandate that all 252 students have authoritative 1:1 mappings established from `scripts/excel_mapping.csv`, and that `STUDENT_REGISTRY` in `src/data/psgimData.ts` and `getTimetableForStudent` resolve accurate dynamic course groups and schedules when called with either College Roll or D-Roll.
2. **Step 2 (Execution vs Ground Truth)**: When `psgimData.ts` was examined, only `26AB12` had its course mapping synchronized with its real D-Roll (`D26AB05`). All other college roll entries (`26AA01`..`26AD60`) were naively keyed to `D26AA01`..`D26AD60`, stripping the prefix without following the excel mapping.
3. **Step 3 (Impact on Scheduling Engine)**: In `getTimetableForStudent(rollNo)`, the lookup checks `STUDENT_REGISTRY[cleanRoll]` first. Because `STUDENT_REGISTRY['26AA04']` exists with `D26AA04`'s courses, calling `getTimetableForStudent('26AA04')` returns Sree Arul S's schedule instead of Akash Vardhaman's schedule. This discrepancy affects 236 out of 252 students (93.6% of the cohort).
4. **Step 4 (Test Inadequacy)**: `tests/e2e_college_roll_audit.cjs` passed 99/99 because it only tested Devdarshan V (`26AB12`) and `26AE01` for schedule and registry generation, creating a false impression of full cohort compliance.
5. **Step 5 (Policy Compliance)**: Item 5 of the dispatch requires Zero Emoji Policy compliance across all updated files. An automated Unicode scan revealed that `src/services/disputeReportService.ts` contains 5 Unicode emojis in `summaryText`.
6. **Step 6 (Conclusion Derivation)**: Because 236 students receive erroneous schedules and an updated service violates the Zero Emoji Policy, the implementation cannot be approved for release in its current state.

## 4. Conclusion

**Verdict: REQUEST_CHANGES**

The College Roll Number migration succeeded for Devdarshan V and UI screen display uniformity, but failed critical cohort-wide verification:
1. **[Critical] Cohort Timetable Discrepancy in `psgimData.ts`**: Re-index `STUDENT_REGISTRY` so that for all 252 students in `scripts/excel_mapping.csv`, `STUDENT_REGISTRY[collegeRollNo]` points to the exact same dynamic course record as `STUDENT_REGISTRY[dRollNo]`, or update `getTimetableForStudent` to normalize `rollNo` via `toOldRollNo(rollNo)` before querying `STUDENT_REGISTRY`.
2. **[Critical] Zero Emoji Policy Violation in `disputeReportService.ts`**: Strip all Unicode emojis (`👤`, `🏫`, `📅`, `🕒`, `⚠️`) from `src/services/disputeReportService.ts` lines 210–221.
3. **[Major] Remove Hardcoded Auth Bypass in `AuthScreen.tsx`**: Replace the hardcoded `isDevdarshanMatch` condition in lines 108–112 with standard roster password lookup.
